                Ben Millard is a young artist from Cincinnati, Ohio who specializes in painting
            and illustration, among other mediums. He is currently attending the University of 
            Cincinnati's College of Design, Architecture, Art, and Planning (DAAP) where he is working 
            towards a Bachelor's Degree is Fine Art, with a concentration in art education. Besides 
            the fine arts, Ben also loves music and media in all of their varieties. He plays the 
            guitar and piano when he can, and enjoys studying the Russian language.
