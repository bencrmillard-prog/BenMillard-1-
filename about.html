I am an artist from Cincinnati, Ohio who specializes in painting and illustration, among other mediums. I am currently attending the University of Cincinnati's College of Design, Architecture, Art, and Planning (DAAP) where I am working towards a Bachelor's Degree in Fine Art, with a concentration in art education. Besides the fine arts, I also love music and media in all of their varieties. I play the guitar and piano when I can, and enjoy studying the Russian language. Recently, I have gotten interested in code, so I took it upon myself to create this website using HTML! I felt it was a rewarding project that expanded my skill set while establishing a hub for myself and my work.
